---
title: And so the moon beckons
tagline: A Halloween One-Shot
---

The moon hangs low and swollen. Criers run the halls of the barracks, pleading:

> "Don't look outside. Don't look at the moon."

In the town square, the villagers prowl and wail, begging to be let indoors.

You are vagrants and passersby, along with the odd local fool brave enough to sign on, all hired on short contracts to stand watch over Wickermoor Village. Most of you owe it nothing. Tonight that bargain sours. From the bell tower, the bells ring out unbidden, and beneath them a desperate voice calls down for help. He claims he can reach whatever power has seized the town, and that he can end this nightmare. He only needs someone to get to him.
