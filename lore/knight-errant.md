---
name: The Knight-errant
aka: Faylese Naal
epithet: Paladin (Oath of the Ancients)
---

A foolhardy warrior of the light, the Knight-errant set out to cleave the darkness from Wickermoor Hollow one good deed at a time. That was the plan, anyway. For most of her time in Wickermoor Village, her "quests" have been couriering parcels from one end of town to the other and fixing creaking doors and wobbly tables for the elderly.

Then a mutated beast came prowling at the village's edge, where new homes had crept too close to its nest. She took up her sword and went out to slay it. Or so the village believes. In truth, the beast carried its young clinging to its body, and in an earnest, clumsy battle, her blow found the child instead. The beast, stricken with grief, fled with its crippled young back into the Wytchwood. She can only hope it never returns.

After the fight, the young beast's black, ichor-like blood seeped into her skin. A searing pain followed, and she was sure it would be her end. Instead she rose with a strange, fae-like magic coursing through her, able to summon spirit-like wings.

Plucky errand girl yesterday, vessel of power beyond her understanding today, she now stands guard over the townsfolk. But her magic keeps drawing her back toward the Wytchwood. Is it some ploy of the grieving beast, or her own new fae nature calling her to meet her kin?
