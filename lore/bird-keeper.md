---
name: The Bird Keeper
epithet: Artificer (Alchemist)
---

A hermit from the nearby Wytchwood, or so she claims. No one has ever heard of a human venturing into those woods and returning unharmed, much less living there. Yet every few days she comes stooping in through the village gate: long-armed, wrapped in a ragged patchwork cloak, and smelling of woodsmoke and strange herbs.

She says she comes to trade, offering potions and poultices for bread, thread, and news. When in down, she has a way of turning up wherever the governor's men are talking. Ask her what lies in the Wytchwood, and she'll only smile: "Trees, dearie. Ever so many trees."

The bustle of the (small) village overwhelms her. She is warm to any pariah, animal, or child she meets. She has endless patience for the Wretched, who adores her for it. But she has a distain for folks that set themselves above others. Oddly, the only member of the guard she greets like an old friend is its gruff founder, the Relentless.

Black-headed sparrows come and go from her shoulders at all hours. They are her confidants, and she seems to understand every chirp. She guards them closely and lets no one else near them.

Though she hardly has a warrior's build, the Bird Keeper fights with an arsenal of potions brewed from whatever her birds bring her: draughts that mend, smokes that blind, sometimes just an empty bottle to throw at someone's head!

## Secret

The Bird Keeper does live in the Wytchwood, and she does not live alone. Her son is there with her, or what the Wytchwood has made of him: a hulking, twisted beast. She could not stop the change, so she did the only thing left to her. She tamed him, and she kept him. She still reads to him every night, and some nights he still listens.

She came to the village to be the woman on the inside: to turn the guard and the governor from their expansion before it reaches her son. But he has learned to follow her. More than once he has come to the barracks looking for her, and the guard has raised the alarm at a monster at the walls. Only the Relentless knows the truth, and only he helps her lead her son home.

Her sparrows are how she manages it. Some of them never leave her son's side, so she always knows where he wanders. When he turns toward the village, a sparrow reaches the Relentless before any bell is rung. Others perch on the sills of the governor's hall, and bring her every word spoken there.
