name: The Relentless
epithet: Ranger (Gloom Stalker)
summary: The gruff hunter who founded the village guard and leads its hunts into the Wytchwood. He resents the governor's expansion, and is grudgingly best friends with the Exiled.
---

An honorary local, the Relentless has lived in Wickermoor Hollow for over a decade. Or rather, in the Wytchwood just outside it.

A proud and brutal hunter, he founded the village's guard and leads its hunts into the forest. Without him, few would find their way home through the dense woods, much less get the drop on the beasts that live there.

In his heart, he has claimed Wickermoor Village as a second chance to defend a home. Years ago, cultists of Olamarsh, the land of the Deepborn, overran the hamlet where he lived with his extended family and carried most of them off for sacrifice. He escaped with only his wife and youngest son, and swore that here, they would know peace again.

When the Exiled arrived, the villagers took one look at the two of them and decided they'd be the best of friends. The Relentless found this deeply annoying, right up until it turned out to be true. He admires the Exiled's resourcefulness and finds his dreams of a grand future somewhere between inspiring and amusing.

He has nothing but disdain for the governor's insistence on pushing the village outward. The forest is so clearly fighting back. Worse, the governor, a weak-minded man, has made him into a puppet through which to play warrior. After so many years, the Relentless sometimes thinks of taking his family and the best of the guard and leaving.

## Secret

The beast that keeps coming to the barracks is the Bird Keeper's son, and the Relentless has been helping her hide him.

He could not save his own children from the cultists. He will not let the guard he built put a spear through someone else's. So when the beast comes looking for his mother, the Relentless goes too, helping her lead him home through the dark, calming him, keeping him safe.

And while he is gone, the guard he built is coming apart. Watches go unorganized, the walls go thin, and the monster attacks grow bolder. Every villager hurt on a night he was away is a weight he carries alone.
