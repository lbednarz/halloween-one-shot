---
name: The Exiled
epithet: Barbarian (Subclass)
---

The Exiled once fought in a rebellion to overthrow the rulers of his homeland. Cornered in a skirmish and nearly killed, he surrendered and, in desperation, joined the very force he had fought against. Months later the rebellion won. He had switched sides just in time to lose.

His former commanding officer recognized him and chose humiliation over execution. The Exiled was stripped of his equipment, shaved bare, and sent stumbling into the woods, doing his best to cover himself.

With nothing, he fought wayward highwaymen and bandits, losing fight after fight. Gradually, he hardened into an immovable, powerful warrior in his own right.

Now he wears his victories. His scale mail is made from the torn scraps of his foes' armor, each scale cut from the breastplates, leathers, and plates of those he's slain, and his greatsword is hung with their cloths, bandannas, and colorful keepsakes. Its sheath holds a set of throwing daggers.

He is self-assured, and his conversations are often short, save for after a good hunt and in fine company. Pragmatic and strong-minded, the Exiled may yet become a great warleader. Will he return for revenge with a warband at his back, live out his days as a mercenary, or put down the sword and retire to a greener countryside?

## Secret

The Exiled is dying. His last great victory was against a bandit warlord whose sword bore an old and hungry magic, and before the warlord fell, that blade bit deep into the Exiled's side. The wound never closed. A black poison has crept from it ever since, and it consumes not his body but his mind: lost hours, faces he should know, a voice beneath his thoughts that is not his own.

He has begun to understand that the warlord was never the sword's master, only its last victim. The blade had hollowed the man out long ago, and when it met the Exiled, it found what it had been searching for: a younger, stronger vessel. He did not win that fight so much as he was chosen.

The voice belongs to a fallen fae, once a servant of the Green Mother in the age when she was at her most benevolent. Now the fae is bound within the blade, sworn to find a host strong enough to level the land the Green Mother still clings to. The greatsword on his back is its prison and its weapon. He took it as his greatest trophy, and now he cannot set it down. When it leaves his reach, the wound burns and the voice grows louder. Each time he rages, he feels the poison creep a little further, and he comes back a little less of himself.

He did not come to Wickermoor for the guard's pay. He came because of the rumors: soil that heals and changes what it touches, and a Green Mother who grants what is asked of her. He will take any means to survive. He does not know that the voice wanted to come here, too.
