name: The Wretched

summary: A riddling, oddly unkillable creature the guard uses as live bait. He dreams of a home, and treasures the Bird Keeper's patience.
---

A creature that is... certainly in the village. He fancies himself a guard, a protector. He has been barred from gate duty ever since he began demanding that travelers "answer thee my riddles three." Each time someone answered, he would reply, "I don't know, maybe!"

He has found some use among the guard as live bait. Beasts from the Wytchwood come to maul him, and somehow, every time, he survives. He claims he has nine... HUNDRED lives.

Everywhere he goes, it's the same story: people mostly just wait for him to leave. He is an excitable conversationalist with little sense for social cues, rambling from one thought to the next in a cheerful stream of consciousness. Only the Bird Keeper has found the seemingly endless patience his company requires, and he treasures her for it.

But the Wretched has big dreams. He wants a home: a door with his name on it, a warm spot by a fire, and a village that's glad to see him come back. Until then, he fights for one. He is a slippery, adaptable combatant who can wriggle out of almost anything, and he lands a brutal backstab whenever he'd like to hang on to one of his lives.

## Secret

The Wretched was not always wretched. Long ago, he was someone's cat here in Wickermoor Village, dozing in a windowsill on a night much like this one, when a low, swollen moon rose over the rooftops. He looked. He remembers none of it. But somewhere in the village, a voice calls to him, a promise of a home, and tonight the moon has come back.
