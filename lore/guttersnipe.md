A meek creature, the Guttersnipe is a wondrous wizard, if an ill-regarded one. Caught thieving and bound for the workhouse, they were taken under the wing of a wizard who saw a brighter future in them. The Guttersnipe proved a fast learner and a do-gooder at heart. Where once they had fought tooth and nail for their next meal, they now spent their nights lost as often in great works of fiction as in their studies.

But the townsfolk they grew up among would not change their hearts. The Guttersnipe is a Tabaxi, a people long pigeonholed as vagrants, peddlers of shoddy goods, and carnival hands. However learned and upstanding the Guttersnipe became, their neighbors had stuck a label on them that would not come off. Not in that town.

And so the Guttersnipe wandered. They delighted in spelunking for forgotten tomes, having spirited conversations, and in perfecting their magic. For a time, it was enough. Then came the aimlessness, and with it a feeling: each time they ventured below the surface, they sensed something stirring beyond the veil.

They have felt it again here, in Wickermoor Village, stronger than ever. Something vast is pressing against the edges of this place, and soon it will break through. The Guttersnipe has told no one. They tell themselves it is because no one would believe a Tabaxi from the gutter. The truth is worse: some part of them wants to see it.

## Secret

The Guttersnipe and the Stargazer know something no one else does: Wickermoor Village is about to be attacked by a great eldritch power. After weeks playing guard, the Guttersnipe is about to glimpse the unthinkable, the unknowable. Their mind is racked with indecision. What great magics might be worth an eldritch incursion? And will a glimpse beyond the material tear their mind asunder?
