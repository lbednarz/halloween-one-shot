name: The Sorceress

---

An ambitious bender of the arcane, the Sorceress has come to Wickermoor Hollow to uncover the nature of the magic that saturates its soil, and of the curious patron the villagers worship there. They speak of the "Green Mother," a distant, Mother Nature-like deity. The Sorceress doubts that any single entity could be behind such an anomaly. She suspects the source is something far older, and far less kind.

Late in life, her father discovered he could not have children. In desperation, her parents struck a deal with the emissary of a mysterious, magical figure for a daughter of their own blood. The child they received was a Tiefling, a rare people widely considered a bad omen. What's more, she was born with the arcane flowing through her, wielding dangerous magic from a very young age.

Even so, her parents are thankful for her and love her dearly. They kept her largely sheltered from the world's prejudice, and when she did meet hardship, they gave her careful guidance and strong tools for facing it.

Arcane by nature, the Sorceress shapes magic in ways that push the boundaries of what people believe it can do, and she rains destruction upon her foes. She takes no pleasure in doing harm, but the chance to exercise her gifts can be exhilarating.

## Secret

The Sorceress suspects that the emissary her parents bargained with served the Green Mother. Her parents have never told her what they promised in return.

Her gifts reach their peak in destruction. When she unleashes it, she feels a hunger stir within her, and it strains her to call on the arcane's gentler or more whimsical sides.
