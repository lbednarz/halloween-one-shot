---
name: The Stalwart
epithet: Paladin (Oath of the Watchers)
---

The Stalwart was born to be a guard, at least in his own mind. His father was a guard, his grandfather was a guard, and now it is his turn. One problem: the Stalwart never grew past an acorn. His relatives grew into mighty, treant-like beings, while he remains small of body, if brave of heart.

He is a tough nut, indeed. He puts on a gruff demeanor, addresses everyone as "citizen," and invents grand, heroic quests where there are none. These often land him in genuine danger, and his quest becomes a rescue mission for the rest of the village.

He was sent to Wickermoor Village because it was quiet. Perhaps there he would not be too disruptive, and might learn some humility and nuance in how he treats people. Besides, an old friend of his grandfather lives there. You can't miss him: he's a giant tree.

Since arriving, he has indeed learned much about people and about his own strengths. He shrugs off the wild blows of the monsters that attack the village, he keeps spirits high when things look bleak, and he truly, deeply cares for the people he tries to protect.

He fights with a halberd, using its reach to hamstring attackers and stand sentinel against any who come too close.

## Secret

The Stalwart is secretly terrified that his family will never accept him as he is, and that in the end he will be unable to protect the people he cares for. He has recurring nightmares in which he is the villagers' last line of defense, and he is easily, humiliatingly defeated and tossed aside.

He does not know why he never grew. His family drank deep of the old soil of the Hollow, and it made them mighty. He never did. Of all his kin, he is the only one the Green Mother has no roots in.
