## The truth

**The Green Mother was once a fair god.** In an earlier age she was benevolent, with servants among the fae, and the villagers of Wickermoor still worship her as a distant, Mother Nature-like deity.

**She was defeated by Philip Druskenvald,** the plane's new ruler and its namesake. Weakened, and filled with mounting hatred for what the plane has become, she allowed an eldritch force to elevate her magic, at the cost of partly corrupting her mind.

**She is now half fae, half eldritch:** voice-tormented, paranoid and power-hungry. She has let the eyes of the eldritch suffuse the soil and air of Wickermoor Hollow, the last domain she holds. She means to retake Druskenvald, starting with an attack on Wickermoor Village. She is the force pressing against the edges of the village, and the source of the strange magic in the soil.

**Not all her servants followed her.** At least one fae has fallen and turned against her (see the Exiled).

**The monsters are Wickermoor's missing villagers,** changed by what the Green Mother has let into the soil and air. The Pomander has proof; the Wall has a single ring.

**The moon has risen like this before.** The Wretched is proof: he was a village cat who looked at the moon the last time.

## Places

- **Wickermoor Hollow**: the province.
- **Wickermoor Village**: the town where the one-shot takes place. Recently expanded toward the Wytchwood.
- **The Wytchwood**: the forest beside the village. Few return from it.
- **Olamarsh**: land of the Deepborn. Its cultists overran the Relentless's home hamlet and took his family for sacrifice.

## People

- **The governor**: a weak-minded man pushing Wickermoor Village outward toward the Wytchwood, against the forest's clear resistance. Uses the Relentless as a puppet to play warrior through. Secretly sick with the early stages of the villagers' change; kept lucid by the Pomander's tincture. His expansion is what put the village near the beast's nest (see the Knight-errant).

## Character threads

- **Sorceress**: born of a bargain her parents made with an emissary, likely of the Green Mother. The price is unknown, even to her. Feels a hunger when she destroys.
- **Guttersnipe & Stargazer**: the only ones who know an eldritch attack is coming. The Guttersnipe secretly wants to see it.
- **Knight-errant**: crippled a mutated beast's young; its black blood gave her fae magic that pulls her toward the Wytchwood. The grieving beast is still out there, and the village thinks she killed it.
- **Flagellant**: secretly murdered her cruel knight mentor in his sleep; the Wytchwood's soil (the Green Mother's) turned his body into the living thorn crown on her head. What's left of him hates her mercy: the thorns tighten and draw blood every time she spares a foe. They've grown since the Green Mother's power swelled; the voice she hears at night is his, carrying whatever is whispering to the maddened villagers. (Publicly, players know only that she hears something too.) Publicly, she's in Wickermoor undoing the damage the knight did here, and has noticed villagers wailing, beating their heads on the gates, and biting.
- **Wretched**: moon-turned cat. A voice in the village promises him a home.
- **Squire**: killed the noble lord she cooked for after he publicly laughed at her wish to be his squire. Her helmet is the dead lord's, disguised with a red plume; anyone from his household who looked closely would recognize it. Her would-be mentor, the Flagellant, also secretly killed the man she squired for (the Squire's secret hints at this), but doesn't know the Squire's story. Every time the Flagellant declines to take her on, it echoes the laugh.
- **Bird Keeper**: really does live in the Wytchwood, with her son, whom the forest turned into a hulking beast. She tamed him and still reads to him. She's in the village to turn the guard and governor from expansion. Her son keeps following her to the barracks. Her sparrows are her spy network: some stay with her son, some carry warnings to the Relentless, some listen at the governor's hall. The Wretched's only real friend.
- **Relentless**: founder of the guard. Secretly helps the Bird Keeper lead her son home whenever he comes to the barracks, because he couldn't save his own children. His absences are why the guard is falling apart and the attacks are getting worse. Friends with the Exiled.
- **Exiled**: dying of a wound from a cursed greatsword, now the blade on his back. Its previous host was the bandit warlord he killed; the sword chose the Exiled as a younger, stronger vessel. It holds a fallen fae, once a servant of the Green Mother, sworn to find a host strong enough to level the land she clings to. The poison eats his mind, and raging speeds it. He came to Wickermoor to bargain with the Green Mother for a cure; the fae wanted to come here too.
- **Stalwart**: an acorn from a family of treant-like tree-folk. His kin grew mighty from the Hollow's soil; he never drank of it, so the Green Mother has no roots in him, and may be unable to touch him. His grandfather's old friend, a giant tree, lives in the village.
- **Summoner**: the demon in her flask is her brother's soul bound into a long-dead demon's body; the two share one mind. Her rituals keep him uppermost; the old bloodbaths were the demon winning. She's right that the Wytchwood's monsters were once gentler things.
- **Stargazer**: Gnarlborn, one of the tree-folk said to be born of the Green Mother. Knows her full story and the coming attack (as does the Guttersnipe). His divine light comes from her, and he fears he's already hers. Hasn't bloomed this year. His partner is alive.
- **Swordsaint**: carries the spirits of her lineage in her cloak, one per sword. Hunting ancient swords lodged in the Wytchwood's eldest monsters, which puts her at odds with the Summoner, Bird Keeper, Relentless and Knight-errant. Some of her mentors' voices seem compromised. On their orders she crippled a guard, and the monsters struck his empty post that night; the Relentless wrongly blames himself. There is one more voice in her cloak than there should be.
- **Wall**: silkborn who believes he's the guard's secret leader and tells everyone. Really a military reject back home; the governor's men send him on every mission because he's expendable. Ambushed at the post left empty by the guard the Swordsaint crippled. A grieving beast gave him a wedding ring belonging to a missing villager, so he knows the monsters are villagers, and has told no one.
- **Pomander**: a real noble, disgraced after a disease he was studying escaped his lab and ravaged his family's lands; the mask is guilt as much as protection. Secretly keeps the sick governor lucid with a tincture, partly to save the village, partly for a promised letter restoring his name. His bestiary proves the monsters are mutated villagers (birthmarks, dental quirks, personal items), that they coordinate, and that their gatherings follow the moon. He has calculated that the greatest gathering is tonight. The change travels through soil and air; his mask has kept him clean.

## Open questions

- Who is the voice in the bell tower, and is he the same voice promising the Wretched a home?
- What did the Sorceress's parents promise in return?
- Is the Knight-errant's beast one of the Green Mother's creatures?
- What happens to those who look at the moon tonight?
- What changed the Green Mother from benevolent to what she is now, and what made her fae servant fall?
- Is the giant tree, the Stalwart's grandfather's friend, one of the Green Mother's?
- Who brokered the Summoner's pact? Her flask bears a circle beneath a horned half-moon: coincidence, or a link to the moon over Wickermoor?
- Where is the Stargazer's partner, and does she know what the Green Mother has become?
- Is the moon one of the "eyes of the eldritch"? It would explain the warning not to look at it.
- The Stalwart is tree-folk too: are Gnarlborn and his family kin?
- **Pinned:** Is the Knight-errant's beast the Bird Keeper's son, and the young it carried her grandchild? If so, the village's hero secretly crippled the Bird Keeper's grandchild.
- Whose is the extra voice in the Swordsaint's cloak: the Green Mother, the eldritch, or the monsters' leader?
- Do the Wytchwood's monsters have a leader coordinating their attacks?
- If the Exiled bargains with the Green Mother, what does the fae in his sword do?
