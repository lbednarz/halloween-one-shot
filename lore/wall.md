A silkborn of considerable size, the Wall sauntered into Wickermoor Village, asked where its strongest fighters stayed, found them at the barracks, and announced that he'd do it: he'd be their leader. After a short conversation with the Relentless, it became clear they were not in search of leadership. But being a modest man and a deep thinker, the Wall understood what the Relentless was *really* saying: the Wall is the leader now, but it must stay secret, so as not to embarrass the Relentless in front of his warriors.

And so the Wall leads the forces of good in Wickermoor from the shadows. Or he would, if he didn't "confide" in a new member of the guard every week. He's cursed with an honest heart. What can he say? He decided to nickname himself "the Stalwart," but that was taken, so "the Wall" had to do. 

Back in his home province, he was the most renowned hero around, a real bad boy, wild at heart, until the glamour grew too much to bear. In Wickermoor, he's treated well and has made many friends in the guard. That was certainly not the case back home. Still, he is a fine warrior. Dense half-plate over a naturally armored body makes him all but impenetrable, and with four arms, he swings a kanabō in each pair.

A few nights ago, he was ambushed by a raiding party of beasts that had slipped through a post where a guard should have stood, but inexplicably didn't. He slew one. The other dropped beside it, wailed in grief at the moon, and knelt by its fallen comrade until he slew it, too.

For once, the Wall hasn't told anyone the whole story.

## Secret

The Wall was never famous. Back home, he was a military reject, laughed out of every barracks he tried to join for dreaming of being the lone hero of the old ballads. The glamour he fled was ridicule. And the reason he is sent on the most dangerous missions is not that he's the best. The governor's men simply think he's the easiest to lose. He hasn't noticed.

But there is one secret he truly keeps. Before the second beast let him strike, it pressed something into his hand: a wedding ring, engraved with a name. He asked around, quietly, as quietly as he has ever done anything. It belonged to a villager reported missing last month.

He knows now what the monsters are. The man who cannot keep a secret has told no one.
