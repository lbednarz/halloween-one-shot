A Gnarlborn, one of the tree-folk, the Stargazer has made pilgrimage to the Wytchwood from lands afar many times before. This time, upon reaching Wickermoor Hollow, something stopped him, suddenly and wordlessly, and he has not gone further. In legend, his kind were born of the Green Mother herself, and the townsfolk revere him for it. They welcomed him with open arms.

The Stargazer reads the heavens for guidance above the motives of mortals. He is expert at discerning people's intent, but he is preoccupied with what is to come for this place. Something here is wrong, and he will not say what. Lately, he will not look at the moon.

By this time of year he should be in bloom, with delicate white-pink flowers that stand in striking contrast to his partner's bold, red-rimmed blossoms. He has not bloomed. He misses her, and tries his best to give the people around him the warmth he would give her, as she would want him to.

Wielding light and the life force of the soil, the Stargazer brings restoration and clarity to his allies, and can channel that light into a purifying force when needed. He forms strong bonds with good-hearted people, and defends them fiercely.

## Secret

His creator is not well. The Green Mother's power has grown vastly since his last pilgrimage, and grown into something macabre. He can feel her breathing in the earth beneath him, feel her pulling him below the surface. Where once there was grace, there is only a grasping hunger. No. This cannot be her.

But he knows what has happened. She was defeated by Philip Druskenvald, the plane's new ruler, whose name it now bears. Weakened, and consumed by hatred for what the plane has become, she allowed an eldritch force to lift up her failing magic, and it cost her part of her mind. The once-fair god is now voice-tormented, paranoid, and hungry for power, half fae and half something else, and she has let the eyes of the eldritch seep into the soil and air of the last domain she holds: Wickermoor Hollow. She means to retake Druskenvald, and she means to begin here, with this small, seemingly unimportant village, any day now.

He also knows something he has told no one. His light comes from her. Since he arrived, it has felt heavier, warmer, hungrier. He fears he is already hers.
