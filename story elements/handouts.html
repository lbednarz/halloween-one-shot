Wickermoor Village

Procedures for a Low Moon

By order of Mayor Wendel Somerton and the Wickermoor Watch.
To be nailed to every door. Read aloud to those who cannot read.

  1. Do not look at the moon. Not through glass. Not in still water. Not in a polished blade.
  2. Do not meet the eyes of anyone who has. If a neighbour’s eyes shine silver, look away. They are no longer your neighbour.
  3. Bar every door. Shutter every window. Put out any light that faces the street. Light draws them.
  4. Do not open the door for a familiar voice. Your family is already inside. Count them.
  5. If you must cross the street, go blind. Cover your eyes. Keep one hand on a wall or on the shoulder ahead. Do not stop for anything you hear.
  6. Do not answer anything that calls you by name.
  7. If someone in your house is bitten, tie them in a room of their own before the Witching Hour. Do not untie them, whatever they say.
  8. The bells:
    • Two bells — stay inside.
    • Three bells — the Watch is coming. Be ready to open.
    • Ringing without end — the wall is breached. Go to the cellar.
  9. Pray to the Green Mother. She protects her children.
Wendel Somerton
Mayor of Wickermoor Village
